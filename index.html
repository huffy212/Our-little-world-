A little letter for you

To my favourite person,

If there is one thing I want you to know, it is that I don't see what we have as something to take for granted. Having someone to share life with is a beautiful thing, and I want to treat what we have with the care it deserves.

I know that love is more than sweet words and beautiful moments. It is patience, honesty, effort, accountability, and the willingness to understand each other even when things are not easy.

I cannot promise that every day will be perfect. What I can promise is that I want to keep learning, keep growing, and keep showing up with intention. I want us to build something that feels safe, genuine, and strong enough to grow with us.

Thank you for being part of my world. For the moments we already have, for the ones we are creating now, and for all the ordinary little days that may one day become our favourite memories.

Whatever the future brings, I hope we never stop making room for laughter, honest conversations, new experiences, and the small things that make us who we are together.

Here's to us, and to the story we are still writing.

With love, always. ♡
