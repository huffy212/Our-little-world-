A little letter from Huffy

To my Liora.

If there's one thing I want this little world to remind you of, it's that what we have doesn't need to look like anybody else's story to be meaningful.

I hope we keep finding reasons to laugh, patience when things get difficult, and the courage to understand each other better. I want the ordinary days to matter just as much as the beautiful ones.

Thank you for being part of my life and of this story. I want to keep choosing honesty, effort, kindness, and us— not only when it is easy, but when it takes work too.

I can't promise every page will be perfect. I can promise that what we build should be real, thoughtful, and ours.

Yours, Huffy. ♡
